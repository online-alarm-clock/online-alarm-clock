# Online Alarm Clock

> Online Alarm Clock is a free browser-based collection of time tools. Set alarms, run timers and stopwatches, view world clocks, and convert time zones without installing an app.

The site is available in English and Chinese. Alarm and clock settings are handled in the browser; alarms require the page to remain open and the device to stay awake.

## Online Tools

### Online Alarm Clock
[Online Alarm Clock](https://onlinealarmclock.net/): Set a free alarm online in your browser: choose a time, pick a sound, and keep this tab open until it rings.

![Online Alarm Clock](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8f31f680-3002-453f-ba86-ca06b927a3ab)

Set your online alarm clock in three steps:

1. Check the local time shown on the page, then choose a date and time or use a quick preset for a common time or a few minutes from now.
2. Choose a sound and press Test alarm to check the volume. Set the repeat mode, snooze interval, and ring count if needed, then name the alarm.
3. Press Set alarm and check the target time and live countdown below. Keep the browser tab open and your device awake until the alarm rings.


### Online Timer
[Online Timer](https://onlinealarmclock.net/online-timer):Set a countdown timer online in seconds — free, no sign-up, no download. Pick your time, choose a sound, and let it run.
![Online Timer](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/60d8c93b-ce22-4771-8b3e-59f0abc7ebdf)

#### How do I set an online timer?
Choose a duration and alarm before starting.

1. Enter hours, minutes and seconds, or select a preset. Use Edit to save your own ten presets on this device.
2. Choose an alarm sound and press Test, or select Silent.
3. Press Start. Use Pause and Resume as needed; Reset returns to the original duration.

### Online Stopwatch
[Online Stopwatch](https://onlinealarmclock.net/online-stopwatch): Press Start and go. Accurate to a hundredth of a second, it keeps running when you switch tabs, and it remembers every lap for you.

![Online Stopwatch](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aec886c3-d942-48bb-b831-3d43b054a6ca)

#### What is an online stopwatch?
An online stopwatch is a browser-based tool that counts up to measure a time interval. Start it on a computer, tablet or phone to track hours, minutes, seconds and hundredths of a second, and record each segment with Lap. For an alert at a specific clock time, use Online Alarm Clock For a countdown that alerts you at zero, use Countdown Timer

### Countdown Timer
[Countdown Timer](https://onlinealarmclock.net/countdown-timer): A big, clear countdown clock for whatever you're waiting on — the next 25 minutes, a launch, a birthday, or December 25.

![Countdown Timer](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a05f0b6-4dff-4a79-b313-446127b5c120)

#### What's the difference between a timer and a countdown?
A timer counts down an amount of time you set, such as 10 minutes. A countdown clock can also count down to a fixed moment, such as midnight on January 1. This page does both.
